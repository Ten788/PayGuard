# PayGuard Requirements

Status: Initial approved concept; implementation acceptance pending.

## Mission
Build a zero-upfront-cost, competition-ready AI-assisted
PayPal payment authorization application.

## Requirements
- PG-001: Real PayPal sandbox integration.
- PG-002: AI interprets requests but cannot authorize payments.
- PG-003: Deterministic validation against explicit human approval.
- PG-004: Deny amount, currency, and merchant mismatches.
- PG-005: Prevent replay and duplicate execution.
- PG-006: Fail closed on malformed or ambiguous instructions.
- PG-007: Auditable authorization decisions.
- PG-008: Polished, intuitive user interface.
- PG-009: Zero-cost local AI infrastructure.
- PG-010: Deterministic automated regression tests.
- PG-011: Demonstrate permitted and denied sandbox scenarios.
- PG-012: Public source and sub-three-minute demonstration video.
- PG-013: Competition eligibility, IP, and publication compliance.

## Constraints
- No real payments.
- No paid APIs or services.
- No secrets committed to source control.
- No external publication without explicit approval.
- Original implementation and license-compliant dependencies.
- Follow VERGE governance and proportional verification.
